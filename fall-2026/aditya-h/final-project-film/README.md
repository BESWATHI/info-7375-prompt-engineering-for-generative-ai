A 2–4 minute show-tell pitch: one drawn picture per beat, minimal on-screen text, narration carrying the explanation.
It's a **pitch, not a build**. Nothing in the Failure Lab exists yet except the idea, a blueprint doc and the baseline
chatbot tests in `research/`. Every drawing of the unbuilt product is tagged **constructed** on screen.

## How to rebuild the film
See `BUILD-PROMPT.md`. In short: `python3 make_sheet.py` → Kokoro audio → `./build_scenes.sh` → `art run` → `art final`.
Everything is local and free: Kokoro voice, Manim, Remotion, ffmpeg.
