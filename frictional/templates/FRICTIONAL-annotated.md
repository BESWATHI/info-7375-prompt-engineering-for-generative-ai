## Entry

**Date and what I was working on** *(label retrospective entries — e.g. `2026-10-03 (retrospective, written 10-05)`)*:

**I tried / expected:**
*→ Your attempts (2 pts). The attempt AND the prediction. A wrong prediction scores the same as a right one; a missing one caps the row at 1.*

**What happened:**
*→ Friction and response (2 pts). Name the object — a value, a run, a test, a filename. "It was confusing" names a category, not an experience. A check that found nothing counts. Do not invent difficulty.*

**What I did:**
*→ Friction and response. Include what you abandoned and why. Dead ends are worth more than the fix.*

**What Claude or another person contributed:**
*→ Human and AI contributions (2 pts). Say what you accepted, changed, rejected, or still do not understand. Naming the tool alone is a 1. Prompt excerpts are enough — full transcripts are unnecessary. "No help this session" is a complete answer.*

**What I understand now / still do not understand:**
*→ Learning and uncertainty (2 pts). Name something still open, and your next question or step. "Nothing unresolved" is almost never true.*

**Evidence and next step:**
*→ Traceable process (2 pts). A test name, a commit subject, a preserved run file, a prompt excerpt — something a reviewer can open.*

## Check before submitting

- [ ] Every entry dated; retrospective entries labelled
- [ ] At least one entry names a specific object, not a category
- [ ] At least one thing that did not work, with why you moved off it
- [ ] Every contribution says accepted / changed / rejected / still unclear
- [ ] Something named as still unresolved, with a next question or step
- [ ] Entries point at commits, tests, runs, or prompt excerpts a reviewer can open
- [ ] No secrets or sensitive personal information anywhere in the log
- [ ] File is named `FRICTIONAL.md` and the Canvas version matches the pushed version
